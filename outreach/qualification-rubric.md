# MOTIONX outreach — qualification rubric

A lead is only worth Charles's time if it is unusually relevant. Sending zero
emails in a run is a success when no excellent lead exists. Never lower a
threshold to hit a quota.

## Gate 1 — must meet at least 5 of these 10
1. Independently owned (not a large national chain).
2. A specific decision-maker can be identified.
3. A public business email from an official or clearly authorized source.
4. Recent business activity within ~45 days.
5. Sells a visually marketable product or high-value service.
6. One new customer could justify a $399–$999 creative project.
7. A clear promotion / launch / booking / consultation / seasonal / lead-gen opportunity.
8. A specific, identifiable weakness in the current site, campaign path, or visuals.
9. MOTIONX has a relevant work example or capability.
10. A timely, concrete reason to contact them *now*.

## Automatic rejects
Large chains · no public contact channel · previously contacted · on the
suppression list · no specific MOTIONX opportunity · personalization would
require inventing facts · presence already excellent (unless a distinct campaign
opportunity exists) · sensitive/exploitative/illegal/inappropriate categories.

## Gate 2 — lead score (0–100). Send only if ≥ 80.
| Dimension | Max |
|---|---|
| Service fit | 25 |
| Evidence of a timely opportunity | 20 |
| Economic ability to purchase | 15 |
| Website / campaign gap | 15 |
| Decision-maker accessibility | 10 |
| Public-email confidence | 10 |
| Portfolio relevance | 5 |

## Gate 3 — account thesis (research-thesis-framework.md)
Before scoring personalization, a complete written thesis (sections A–H) must
exist and answer all ten thesis questions in SKILL.md from public evidence. If it
cannot, or disconfirming evidence (H) outweighs the thesis, **reject** — no score
is computed.

## Gate 4 — personalization score (0–10). Proceed only if ≥ 9.
Use the fixed-criteria rubric in `personalization-rubric.md` (six criteria) and
show the point-by-point breakdown. Also run the standalone non-transferability gate
(≥70% reusable on body OR subject → reject) and the decision-maker fit gate
(SKILL.md Step 6b). **Necessary but not sufficient on its own (v2.2).**

## Gate 5 — quality + readiness gates (v2.2). ALL must pass before a draft.
- **Site-wide contradiction check** (SKILL.md Step 5a / framework section I): inspect
  the whole official site before claiming any absence; if unresolved, do not draft.
- **Recipient-certainty gate** (Step 6c): greeting must match what's proven; a first
  name on a general inbox without justification is an automatic failure.
- **Commercial-readiness** (Step 5b / framework section J): the proposed customer
  action must currently be possible.
- **Recipient-empathy editor** (Step 7c): a separate skeptical-founder pass; revise
  once if any answer is problematic.
- **Phone-screen test** (Step 7d): understood in <20s, one visible idea, CTA last.
- **Draft Quality Score ≥ 9** (personalization-rubric.md), set by the editorial pass
  — **not** the research writer alone — plus the **automatic-failure conditions**
  (any one fails the draft regardless of total).

## Evidence rules (source-confidence hierarchy)
- ≥3 meaningful sources when available, incl. ≥1 **Tier 1** primary official source.
- The trigger and primary thesis must rest on **Tier 1** evidence; Tier 3
  (snippets, aggregators, scraped sites) cannot support the central claim alone.
- Every factual claim in the dossier carries its exact URL, source tier, and date.
- Never invent revenue, customer counts, conversion rates, traffic, upcoming
  launches, business problems, owner identity, preferences, or results.
